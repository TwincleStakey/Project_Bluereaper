# Snow plague field (설원 역병 아레나) — credits

## Made for this project
- Kit models in `Models/` (SP_*): dead trees, dead shrubs, frosted grass tufts, stumps, foreground dead branches, red pines
  (적송) with needle cards, red maple bushes, straw mats over the dead (거적), broken handcart and wheel, stakes and fences,
  sacks, rags, clay jars, stone lanterns (석등) with glow core, light-cone mesh, ground scatter (pebbles, stone chips,
  twigs, bones, snow clumps) and snow drifts. Generated in Blender 5.2 by `BlenderSource~/build_kit.py` and
  `build_kit2.py` (shared helpers in `splib.py`). Frost mask, AO and sway are baked into vertex colours.
- Procedural textures (`BlenderSource~/make_textures.py`): SP_FoliageAtlas (pine needles, maple leaves, dry grass,
  twigs), SP_Blood (2x2 decal atlas), SP_Cookie_* (stone lantern light cookies), SP_FogNoise.
- Shaders (`Shaders/`, lit wrappers generated by `BlenderSource~/gen_shaders.py`).
- Work .blend files, downloads and previews stay in `ArtSource/SnowPlague/` (gitignored).

  forest_leaves_03 (leaf litter). Albedo, GL normal and height, 2k.
- Prop textures (1k): pine_bark, bark_brown_02, wood_planks_grey, dark_rock_02.
- Models in `ThirdParty/PolyHaven/` (decimated, frost vertex colours added by `BlenderSource~/convert_ph.py`):

## Redo 2 — vegetation
- Made for this project (no third-party assets): `Models/Veg/SP_V_*` low dome shrubs (Dome_A/B), twiggy shrubs and thickets
  (Brush_A/B, Thicket_A/B), cattail groups (Cattails_A/B/C), reed stands (ReedWall_A/B), wind-bent grass bunches
  (BentGrass_A/B/C), seed-head weeds with snow caps (SeedWeed_A/B), gnarled framing dead trees (FrameDead_A-D) and snowy
  red pines (FramePine_A-C). Generated headless in Blender 5.3 by `BlenderSource~/veg/build_veg.py` (helpers from
  `BlenderSource~/splib.py`).
- `Textures/Veg/SP_V_CardAtlas.png` (pine needles x2, twig spray, dry grass) and `SP_V_StrawPalette.png` (stem colour ramps):
  procedural, `BlenderSource~/veg/make_atlas.js` / `make_palette.js` (Node.js). Colours follow refs2/NOTES.md (observation only).
- Bark textures reused from the Poly Haven set above (bark_brown_02, pine_bark, CC0).
