# QA 02 - Underworld hell boss room, pass2 captures

Type: Visual/Feel, gate BLOCKING. Severities are proposed; qa-lead confirms Blockers. Screen positions are in px on a 1600x900 capture.

1. **Blocker - dead tree in front of the arena.** `-2_-24_z1.6` centre (trunk at x~930, from the top down to y~700, branches across the player) and left (x~200). `-2_-24_z2.6` too. To see it, walk to the south edge of the plaza. The trees on the south rim (HellSet line 703, scale 5.5-7.5) project up over the play area. Fix: drop these trees, or cap them at about 2 m, or fade them with the see-through shader near the camera.
2. **Blocker - lava pool under the player.** `-38_0_z1.6/z2.6`: a wide, white-hot lava pool covers the centre of the screen. Orange bullets and hit VFX would not show over it. Fix: no wide pools on the plaza. Keep rivers thin and use a darker core.
3. **Major - lava rivers too bright and wide for bullets.** In `5_5_z1.6` (lower right), `6_8_z1.6` (centre to lower right) and `38_20_z1.6` (the river runs under the player), the rivers are 40-60 px wide with saturated cores. Fix: cut the emission about 40%, narrow the rivers, and/or push enemy bullets toward magenta or white.
4. **Major - raised portal platform with steps reaches into the plaza.** It sticks about 3 m past the north lip in `-2_18_z2.6` (right, y 190-390), `38_20_z1.6/z2.6` (upper left) and `5_5_z2.6` (top right). The step edges are on walkable ground. Fix: move it north of zMax, or remove its collider and flatten the steps.
5. **Major - floor corner looks cut off at 45 degrees.** `38_20_z1.6`: there is a diagonal floor edge from (740,290) to (1110,490). The NE corner of plazaRect looks like void, but the player can still walk there. Fix: make the floor fill the full rect corner.
6. **Major - blocky lava-sea texture.** Square, pixelated patches show in `-2_18_z1.6` (top band), `-38_0` (left) and `38_20` (right). Fix: raise the texture resolution or use bilinear filtering on UW_LavaSea, or check the UV tiling.
7. **Major - black slabs with hard seams.** In `30_-5_z2.6` (right third, x>1160) and `38_20_z2.6` (x>950), a dark wedge with a hard straight edge cuts the lava rivers off out of alignment. In `-38_0_z1.6` (left, 0-500 px), stepped rectangular floor slabs and a dock stick out of the west edge. Fix: blend the rivers into the plaza edge and remove the rectangular slab pieces.
8. **Major (inferred, check in Scene view) - rim props may float.** Dark boulder blobs sit over the lava in `-38_0`, `38_20_z2.6` (1200,560) and `30_-5_z2.6` (1200,250). `Ground()` returns 0 when the raycast hits nothing, and the sea quad has no collider, so props past the lip land at plaza height, 6 m above the sea. Fix: skip a prop when the raycast misses, or place it at seaHeight.
9. **Major - enemies would be hard to see at the screen edges.** Outside the player's light pool the floor is almost black (`-15_-8`, `-2_-24`, all corners). Dark enemies would not read there. Fix: raise the ambient floor light, or give enemies a rim or outline.
10. **Minor - small lit posts on the plaza.** `-2_18_z1.6` (705,290), `30_-5_z1.6` (940,310), `-38_0_z1.6` (1370,290). They could block movement. Fix: make sure they have no collider, or move them past the lip.
11. **Minor - north plaza edge is a straight ruler line.** `-2_18_z1.6/z2.6`: there are no boulders along it (by design), and the edge reads as a hard seam against the sea. Fix: break up the edge or add a glow line.

The player sprite (dark navy) reads clearly on the grey floor and on the lava in every capture. I found no cyan placeholders or z-fighting.
