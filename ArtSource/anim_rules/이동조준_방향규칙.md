# Move-aim direction rule (WASD legs + cursor upper body), set 2026-11-18 by Kido ("이방향규칙 정해놓고 적용해")

Basis: the V Rising dev write-up (80.lv, "V Rising's Animation Layering in Unity") and common twin-stick / top-down practice
(two layers: a lower-body strafe blend + a masked upper body; the pelvis turns toward the aim).

1. The root never snaps or flips. The body turns continuously: toward the move direction while moving, toward the cursor while standing.
2. Legs = the locomotion layer. Upper body = the attack / aim layer (the mask leaves the legs out).
3. The turn toward the cursor is shared between the PELVIS and the CHEST. The pelvis turns, and the leg animation switches to
   strafe / backpedal for it (MoveX / MoveZ in body space, 2D blend). The feet keep their placement.
4. The pelvis takes most of the turn. The chest alone turns at most 45 degrees (no over-twist like v4's ~100 degrees).
5. The leg clip comes from the angle between the move direction and the pelvis facing:
   forward (within ±45 degrees) / side (45 to 135 degrees) / backpedal (over 135 degrees), blended, with hysteresis at the boundaries so it doesn't flicker.
6. While running, the attack's spine motion is weakened (straighter spine).
7. The weapon never passes through the legs in any combination (checked by a per-frame distance check).
8. The attack itself plays on the upper body whatever the legs are doing.

Implementation for 이기어검 (EogeomCombat.DirRule):
- PelvisDir() = cursor aim leaning at most 30 degrees (PelvisLead) toward the move direction.
  Running straight away from the cursor (over 150 degrees), the last lean side is kept (hysteresis).
- The body turns toward it continuously at 900 degrees/s (PlayerController).
- AimUpper(): one yaw on the Spine bone, limited to ChestMax 45 degrees.
- RunSpineShare 0.65: the attack's spine weight while running.
- LegClear: the per-frame distance from the sword to the leg bones.
Apply the same rule to other weapons (greatsword / 사복검) only after Kido confirms. Those systems belong to other threads.
